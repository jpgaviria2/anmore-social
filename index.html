How this space works

Positive community content is selected by our sources before it reaches the relay. This site does not judge or approve posts. Top zapped is an explicit amount sort, not a positivity score.

Authors need a signed profile with an exact @trailscoffee.com identity that resolves to their public key. That confirms the identity association, not the accuracy of every post.

We read only relay.anmore.me. No account, tracking, or secret keys are needed. Photos may load from external hosts; opening a source link leaves this site.

All post zaps go to the community group, not the authors. Only verified, post-bound Lightning receipts from the loaded relay snapshot count. An unpaid invoice, claimed amount, or generic transfer does not increase a post’s total. Receipt issuers attest payment; these are not independent settlement proofs. Cashu nutzaps are not supported here.
