A community bulletin for the place we call home. No likes to chase, no personalized feed. Newest is the default; you can choose Top zapped to sort loaded posts by verified community tips.

How this space works

Positive community content is selected by our sources before it reaches the relay. This site does not judge or approve posts. Top zapped is an explicit amount sort, not a positivity score.

Authors need a signed profile with an exact @trailscoffee.com identity that resolves to their public key. That confirms the identity association, not the accuracy of every post.

Posts, profiles and deletions come only from relay.anmore.me. Post-linked receipts are also checked on relay.primal.net. No account, tracking, or secret keys are needed. Photos may load from external hosts; opening a source link leaves this site.

Receipt verification

All post zaps go to the community group, not the authors. Only verified, post-bound community Lightning receipts from Anmore and Primal count in this loaded snapshot. A partial check shows provisional observations; totals/order may change as receipts become available. Missing receipts are not verified zero. An unpaid invoice, claimed amount, or generic transfer does not increase a post’s total. Receipt issuers attest payment; these are not independent settlement proofs. Cashu nutzaps are not supported here.
